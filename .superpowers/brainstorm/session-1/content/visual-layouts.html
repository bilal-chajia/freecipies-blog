Premium Admin Redesign: Comprehensive Design System & Layout

Layout is the skeleton of the UI. We are overhaulng the entire admin design system: structural grids, card spacing, border depths, form control alignment, and micro-interactions. Below are the layout and design system choices.

1. Layout & Grid Philosophies

Choose the core structural layout philosophy for the CMS workspace. Click a card to select it.

CRISP 2-PANEL GUTENBERG
RECOMMENDED

Crisp Gutenberg 2-Panel

A structure inspired by the WordPress block editor. Left-side workspace navigation remains static, while settings feature a secondary dedicated navigation rail. Content fills the canvas with zero-padding cards, maintaining high informational density and precise alignments.

ELEGANT FLOATING CARDS
HIGH DEPTH

Floating Card Canvas

A canvas-first aesthetic where individual setting sections live in physical "floating cards" elevated above a neutral off-white or gray background (`#f1f5f9`). Utilizes beautiful premium drop shadows and card margins to convey organization and relief.

FRAMELESS WORKSPACE GRID
ULTRA HIGH DENSITY

Frameless Grid Workspace

A minimalist design resembling spreadsheet tools or dashboard apps. Discards secondary panel background blocks and floating cards entirely in favor of border grids, tight spacing, and direct cell interactions. Extreme space efficiency for power users.
